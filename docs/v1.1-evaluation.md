## Honest Generalization Experiment

A separate conservative fine-tune kept `s00` completely absent from training.
Removing Mosaic, MixUp, random crop, and flip reached mAP 0.123 at epochs 4
and 6 versus the immutable V1 checkpoint's 0.126. Excavator mAP remained 0.
The experiment was stopped as a failed branch and is not promoted.

The result identifies a data-coverage constraint: `s00` contains 91
`back_left` Excavator boxes, while the fold-0 training sequences contain zero
Excavator boxes from that camera. A new independent mine sequence or targeted
reviewed examples from missing views are required before claiming generalized
Excavator improvement.

## Visual Regression

- overlapping Car/Truck/WaterTruck boxes collapsed to one Truck;
- the large `back_left` Excavator and the `back_right` Excavator were detected.

Generated images and weights stay under ignored `artifacts/` directories and
